About Transcribble

We flipped classic drawing games upside down. In this blind-sketch challenge, the artist has no idea what they are drawing.

The rest of the lobby must describe the secret word using their microphones, but beware: our voice recognition is listening for forbidden words.

🎯 Guide the blind artist in Classic Mode, or outsmart the faker in Impostor Mode!
📌

Patch Notes & Updates

  • Single Player Modes: Play solo anytime! Enjoy relaxing symmetry in Zen Mode or test your skills in the brand new Drawing Academy!
  • Drawing Academy(Beta): Master drawing through 4 escalating tiers with instant pixel accuracy scoring and helpful guides!
  • Impostor Mode: One secret faker doesn't know the word! Take turns sketching, vote on suspects, or guess the word to steal the win!
  • Undo Button & Export: Fix any slip-ups instantly with the new canvas undo button, or save your artwork straight to your device!
  • Mobile & Word Bank: Full smartphone layout with quick tabs, plus 100+ fresh categorized words & taboo traps!

How to Play

🎨 Blind Drawer
  • You cannot see the secret prompt.
  • Listen to incoming voice clues & read chat.
  • Draw what the room describes onto the canvas.
  • Type guesses in chat to solve the mystery word!
🚫 Describers
  • You see the secret target word.
  • Hold Spacebar or button for voice clues (up to 3.5s).
  • NEVER say or type the banned trap words!
  • Guide the drawer using geometry & position hints.
💡 Pro Tip: Describe simple shapes (e.g. "draw an oval in the center") rather than the object!
🛡️
PRIVACY & AUDIO NOTICE

Transcribble processes voice audio in real-time solely for in-game speech recognition. Microphone streams are ephemeral, processed in memory, and never recorded or stored to disk. Intended for players aged 13+.

Privacy & Microphone Safety

🎙️ Real-Time Voice Streaming Only

Microphone audio is only streamed during active rounds when you actively hold the voice button or Spacebar. Audio clips are up to 2 seconds long and sent encrypted to our speech recognition engine.

🚫 Zero Audio Storage or Voiceprints

We do not create biometric voiceprints, do not log or store audio files, and do not sell or train AI models with your voice. The audio buffer is immediately freed from memory after transcription.

👥 Temporary Session Data

Player nicknames, room IDs, and in-game scores are kept in memory only for the duration of the match and are permanently deleted when all players leave the room.

🧒 COPPA & Age Compliance

Transcribble is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
